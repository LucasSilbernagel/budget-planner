## Features

Budget Planner is split into a Free tier that runs entirely on your device and
a Premium tier that adds syncing and advanced planning tools.

### Free tier

- **Income and expense tracking** with weekly, biweekly, monthly, and annual
  frequencies.
- **Frequency normalization** — every value is converted to a common monthly
  basis so totals are always comparable.
- **Savings goals** to track progress toward a target.
- **Balance tracking** for investments and debts.
- **Visual breakdowns** of income versus expenses and assets versus
  liabilities.
- **Local-first storage** — your data lives in your browser and never leaves
  your device on the Free tier.

### Premium tier

- **Multi-device sync** so your data follows you across devices.
- **Custom profiles** for separating different financial scenarios.
- **Retirement modeling** using a safe-withdrawal projection.
- **Net-worth projections** that look forward over time.
- **Currency display** with locale-aware formatting, or a currency-less mode
  for raw numbers.

### Privacy and data location

Budget Planner is built around data sovereignty. When you upgrade to Premium,
your synced data is stored on servers located in the European Union. There is
no US data residency, which keeps your information outside the scope of the US
CLOUD Act.
